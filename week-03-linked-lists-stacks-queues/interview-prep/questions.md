# Week 3 Interview Questions

## Linked lists
**Q:** Reverse a linked list.  
**A:** Use prev/current/next pointers; O(n) time, O(1) space.

## Stacks
**Q:** How do you check balanced brackets?  
**A:** Stack of opens; match on close; O(n).

## Queues
**Q:** Why is list.pop(0) slow?  
**A:** It shifts all elements; O(n). Use deque.
