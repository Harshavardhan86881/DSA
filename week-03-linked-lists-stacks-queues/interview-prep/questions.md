# Week 3 Interview Questions

Q: Reverse a linked list.
A: Use prev/current/next; O(n) time, O(1) space.

Q: Detect cycle.
A: Fast/slow pointers; O(n) time, O(1) space.

Q: Valid parentheses.
A: Stack; push opens, match closes; O(n).

Q: Min stack.
A: Parallel min stack; O(1) operations.

Q: Queue using stacks.
A: Two stacks; amortised O(1).

Q: Why deque?
A: O(1) both ends.
