Arrays, strings, hashing, prefix sums. Complexity and problem-solving routine.

## Week 2
Two pointers, sliding window, binary search, binary search on answer, sorting.

Linked lists, stacks, queues, monotonic stack, deque.

Graphs, BFS, DFS, components, topological sort, shortest paths.

Backtracking, greedy, dynamic programming.
