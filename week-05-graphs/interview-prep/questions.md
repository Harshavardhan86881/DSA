# Week 5 Interview Questions

Q: BFS vs DFS?
A: BFS finds shortest unweighted paths; DFS explores fully.

Q: Adjacency list vs matrix?
A: List O(V+E), matrix O(V^2).

Q: How to mark visited?
A: Mark on enqueue to avoid duplicates.

Q: Topological sort validity?
A: Only DAGs.

Q: Number of islands?
A: DFS/BFS over grid; mark visited land.

Q: Shortest path in unweighted graph?
A: BFS levels.
